# Appendices

The five lettered appendices collect material that benefits from being
separate from the narrative but that readers return to frequently: a
role-grouped FAQ, a glossary, a reading list, the HarnessCard template,
and a consolidated ``CLAUDE.md`` sample. The sixth body of appendix-style
material — a 60-minute internal-talk script distilled from the whole
book — has been promoted to **Part 0 · Presentation** at the front of
the book, because it is meant to be *given* rather than *consulted*.
