# Open Questions in Harness Engineering

A copyable list researchers may cite directly.

1. Meta-harness versioning under multi-tenant LLM deployments — how should
   harness rules survive a provider rolling out a model change mid-quarter?
2. When is the right moment to migrate from blog-format `.md` context
   into a structured RAG pipeline? (Karpathy's context-engineering
   framing bears on this.)
3. HarnessCards for polyglot monorepos and cross-geography teams — does
   the 3 × 4 matrix survive scaling up the unit of analysis?
4. Can entropy reduction be measured continuously across a sprint, or is
   milestone-level measurement the highest useful resolution?
5. What is the smallest research benchmark that distinguishes a good
   harness from a bad one on short horizons?
6. How do academic AI-engineering evaluation suites (Terminal Bench,
   SWE-Bench) relate to HarnessCard rubrics — are they measuring the
   same phenomenon at different scales?
7. What role should regulators play in requiring HarnessCard disclosure
   when agents operate in safety-critical contexts?
